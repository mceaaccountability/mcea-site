Have you ever seen people fight so fiercely to keep an unpaid volunteer position?

It relies on a quiet hope: that things get forgotten. That records are erased. That people eventually move on.

That broken trust stays buried.

Numbers, however, possess a cold, unforgiving memory.

What was spent remains spent. What was signed remains signed. And what was quietly moved from one place to another leaves an undeniable trail.

Eventually, someone follows it.

You will be held accountable.
